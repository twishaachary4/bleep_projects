slow coffee

✦for cinematic moments✦

Coffee arrives like a quiet sunrise in a cup, dark as midnight yet carrying the promise of morning. Steam curls upward in slow, silver ribbons, whispering of roasted hills and distant rain. The first sip is a small awakening, bitter and golden at once, warming the hands before it warms the soul. Conversations soften around it, silences feel less lonely, and ordinary hours turn gentle.
